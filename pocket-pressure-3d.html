3D Pocket Pressure

Hold the on-screen movement buttons, or use W/A/S/D or the arrow keys, to move in the pocket. Hold two directions to move diagonally. Your offensive line blocks most defenders, but pressure can break through. Step up, slide, or drop back behind the line of scrimmage before a defender reaches you. You cannot cross the line of scrimmage.
