Progression Reads

Read the receivers in order and throw to the first one who is open (defender has separation). Choose a 3, 4 or 5 receiver concept. Tap a receiver or press its number before the clock runs out. A session is 10 reps.

Coaching cues
  • Read before the drop ends.
  • Throw the first open receiver, not the deepest.
  • Do not peek at #2 before checking #1.
  • Cover 2 leaves the deep middle and the short sidelines soft. Cover 3 leaves the short and medium zones soft.
  • Four Verticals: identify the safety shell first. Against two high, look to the seams. Against one high, look outside. Take the checkdown when everything deep is covered.
  • Flood: read the flat defender. If he widens with the flat, throw the sail behind him. If he sinks under the sail, throw the flat.
