Progression Reads

Read #1, then #2, then #3. Throw to the first receiver who is open (defender has separation). Tap a receiver or press 1, 2 or 3 before the clock runs out. Space starts a rep.

Coaching cues
  • Read before the drop ends.
  • Throw the first open receiver, not the deepest.
  • Do not peek at #2 before checking #1.
  • Cover 2 leaves the deep middle and the short sidelines soft. Cover 3 leaves the short and medium zones soft.
