Progression Reads

Read the receivers in order and throw to the first one who is open (defender has separation). Choose a 3, 4 or 5 receiver concept and a defensive coverage. Tap a receiver or press its number before the clock runs out. A session is 10 reps.

Session setup

Level: 1/10

Level 10 = real pass-rush timing. Levels 1-9 add training time. With the pass rush on, you have about 2.70 seconds at Level 10 before the rush gets home. Each level below 10 adds 0.15 seconds of training time. With the pass rush off, the rep ends as a read timeout, not a sack. The Random concept button picks one 4 or 5 receiver concept for the session. Your score is saved to the Results tab. If players have been added to the Players tab, you can pick one to save the session to the Players tab instead.

Coaching cues
  • Read before the drop ends.
  • Throw the first open receiver, not the deepest.
  • Do not peek at #2 before checking #1.
  • Cover 2 leaves the deep middle and the short sidelines soft. Cover 3 leaves the short and medium zones soft.
  • Four Verticals: identify the safety shell first. Against two high, look to the seams. Against one high, look outside.
  • Flood: read the flat defender. If he widens with the flat, throw the sail behind him. If he sinks under the sail, throw the flat.
  • Yankee: key the middle safety. If he drives on the cross, throw the post. If he runs with the post, throw the cross. If both are capped, check down.
  • Dagger: the dig is the primary read. If a hook defender sinks under it, the curl opens. The flat is the outlet.
  • Levels: read the hook defender. If he drops under the dig, throw the shallow in. If he sits on the in, throw the dig.
  • Snag: scan from the arrow to the snag to the corner, then the backside.
  • Mesh: against man, hit the drag coming clean out of traffic. Against zone, work corner, sit, drag, back.
