Red-Zone Decisions

The field is short, the windows are tight and one bad throw costs points. Read the receivers in order and throw to the first open one. If nobody is open, scramble only when the lane is clear (a green arrow shows it). If a defender is spying the lane, throw the ball away. Never force it into coverage. A session is 10 reps.

Session setup

Level: 1/10

Level 10 uses a simulated 3.15-second rush arrival, not a measured guarantee of real protection. Each easier level adds 0.15 seconds of training time. Route development is independent of pressure timing. With pass rush off, all coverages use a 5-second read timeout, not a sack. Each rep starts at a random spot inside the 20 with a random down. About one rep in three has nobody open, so decide between a scramble and a throwaway. Your score is saved to the Results and Progress tabs. If players have been added to the Players tab, you can pick one to save the session to that player instead.

Coaching cues
  • Before the snap, find the best matchup, find the extra defender and know your second answer.
  • Throw the first open receiver. Do not wait for a better one.
  • A throwaway is a good play. An interception in the red zone steals points.
  • Scramble only when the lane is open. A spy takes the lane away.
  • Fades and corners lose space near the goal line. Quick routes like slants, sticks and flats gain value.
  • Man coverage and Cover 0: crossing routes (mesh) get free from traffic. Cover 3 and Cover 4: take the underneath routes.
